Ok, small thing that had been annoying me. I use [oh-my-zsh](https://ohmyz.sh/), and when you `ssh n` it autocompletes based on known hosts not what's in your `~/.ssh/config`

I added the [following to my dotfiles](https://github.com/icco/dotFiles/blob/main/link/zshrc#L64-L69):

```sh
# Better SSH/Rsync/SCP Autocomplete
# _ssh_hosts skips ~/.ssh/config whenever known_hosts matches, so feed config Host aliases in directly
zstyle -e ':completion:*:(ssh|scp|rsync|sftp):*' hosts 'reply=(); local f l; for f in ~/.ssh/config(N) ~/.orbstack/ssh/config(N); for l in ${(f)"$(<$f)"}; [[ $l == (#i)[[:space:]]#host[[:space:]]##* ]] && reply+=(${${=l}[2,-1]:#(*[*?]*|!*)})'
zstyle ':completion:*:(scp|rsync):*' tag-order ' hosts:-ipaddr:ip\ address hosts:-host:host files'
zstyle ':completion:*:(ssh|scp|rsync):*:hosts-host' ignored-patterns '<->.<->.<->.<->' '*:*' loopback ip6-loopback localhost ip6-localhost broadcasthost
zstyle ':completion:*:(ssh|scp|rsync):*:hosts-ipaddr' ignored-patterns '^(<->.<->.<->.<->|(|::)([[:xdigit:].]##:(#c,2))##(|%*))' '127.0.0.<->' '255.255.255.255' '::1' 'fe80::*'
```

And now #ssh host aliases in `.ssh/config` take precedence!
